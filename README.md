## 🇪🇸 ¿Qué es Kovni AI?

**Kovni** es un asistente de IA personal con **memoria persistente**. A diferencia de ChatGPT u otros LLMs que olvidan todo entre conversaciones, Kovni te recuerda: tus preferencias, tu contexto, tu día a día.

Se integra profundamente con **Google Workspace** (Calendar, Gmail, Tasks, Drive, Contacts) y opera a través de múltiples canales: web, Telegram y próximamente WhatsApp.

## 🇬🇧 What is Kovni AI?

**Kovni** is a personal AI assistant with **persistent memory**. Unlike ChatGPT or other LLMs that forget everything between sessions, Kovni remembers you: your preferences, your context, your day-to-day life.

It deeply integrates with **Google Workspace** (Calendar, Gmail, Tasks, Drive, Contacts) and operates through multiple channels: web, Telegram, and WhatsApp (coming soon).

---

## 🎯 The problem we're solving

LLMs forget everything between sessions. You waste time re-explaining context, repeating your preferences, and pasting the same information over and over. Your AI should know you.

**Kovni does.**

---

## ✨ Key features

- 🧠 **Persistent memory** — semantic vector search over your facts and context (pgvector)
- 📅 **Google Workspace integration** — Calendar, Gmail, Tasks, Drive, Contacts
- 💬 **Multi-channel** — web app + Telegram bot (WhatsApp coming soon)
- 🎙️ **Voice notes** — speech-to-text on Telegram via Whisper
- 🖼️ **Vision** — send photos and get contextual answers
- 🔄 **Multi-provider LLM chain** — xAI Grok primary, Anthropic Claude + Google Gemini fallback
- 🌍 **Multilingual** — Spanish, English, Dutch, French
- 🔐 **Privacy-first** — GDPR compliant, AES-256-GCM encrypted OAuth tokens
